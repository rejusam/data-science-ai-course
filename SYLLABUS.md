# Syllabus

Twenty-five weeks, from first principles to a deployed capstone project.

Sessions run Monday and Wednesday evenings, 6-8pm AEST (8-10pm NZST),
with a full-day Saturday intensive every second week, 10am-4pm AEST
(12pm-6pm NZST). Optional support runs 30 minutes before and after every
session.

This table follows the course delivery plan. If a date here disagrees with the
official timetable, the official timetable wins.

## By the numbers

- 60 teaching sessions in total
- 13 Saturday intensives
- 4 non-teaching dates (public holidays and the end-of-year break)
- Attendance requirement is 90%, which is 54 sessions. That leaves 6 you can miss.

GitHub repository with a README. The capstone is presented in the final
