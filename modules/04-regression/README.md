- Interpret the coefficients in plain English, not just the R-squared
- Watch every other presentation and write one thing to steal and one to avoid
- Start your LinkedIn/GitHub tidy-up now, not in week 24
- Deliberately overfit a model once so you recognise the symptom instantly
