## How to get the most out of these sessions

- Sit the quiz closed-book; use the gaps to direct your revision
- Practise explaining model choice as if to a non-technical manager
- Book your clinic slot early; come with a specific question
- Rebuild one ensemble model from a blank notebook
- Prepare a 60-second answer for 'tell me about a project you built'
- Get your GitHub reviewed by a peer and act on the feedback
- State your model's limitations openly - it signals maturity
- Rehearse to time, out loud, at least three times
- Bring your rehearsal recording and self-critique it first
- Have a backup: screenshots in case the live demo fails
- Ask for written feedback and a reference while your work is fresh in everyone's mind
