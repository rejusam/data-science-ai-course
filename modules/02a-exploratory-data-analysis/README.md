- EDA continued: time-series and geospatial data, feature engineering

## Labs and activities

- Labs 2.1.1 & 2.1.2 (bikeshare.csv, iris); critical-thinking case discussion
- Lab 2.1.3

## How to get the most out of these sessions

- Present your EDA findings in 3 slides - start building presentation muscle from week 4
- Keep a personal 'EDA checklist' notebook you reuse in every project
