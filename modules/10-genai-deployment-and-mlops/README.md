## How to get the most out of these sessions

- Keep a prompt library with the outputs that worked
- Ship something small end-to-end; a working demo beats a perfect notebook
- Deploy your best MP model and add the live link to your CV
- Sketch the full lifecycle diagram for your capstone
- Agree your scope before the break so the holidays are productive, not stressful
- Be able to say when NOT to use Spark - judgement is the skill
